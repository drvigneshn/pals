Which device is this?

You use two screens: a big one the team watches, and your phone to control it. Open this page on both.

1. Monitor on the laptop→2. a QR code appears→3. scan it with your phone

What it is

PALS Companion turns any laptop or projector into a simulated paediatric bedside monitor: ECG, pleth, capnography and respiration waveforms with HR, SpO₂, EtCO₂, RR, BP and temperature, alarms and sound. You control everything live from your phone, so a manikin, a mock code or a viva becomes a realistic resuscitation scenario.

No accounts, no installation, nothing to buy. It works in the browser and keeps working offline once opened.

How to use it

  1. On the laptop or projector, open pals.pediaos.com and tap Monitor. A QR code and a 4-letter code appear.
  2. On your phone, scan the QR code (or open the site, tap Instructor and type the code). The phone header turns green: Monitor online.
  3. On the monitor, tap Start monitor to turn on the sound.
  4. On your phone, open Scenario, pick a case, read the stem to the team and press Start. Move through the stages with Next stage while you tick what the team does.
  5. Use Live for instant changes: rhythm and breathing quick actions, the step-by-step Cardiac arrest panel (compressions → defibrillator → ROSC), drugs given, and ⚡ Surprise complications.
  6. At the end, open Debrief: time to CPR, time to first shock, adrenaline timing, CPR fraction and the full timeline. Show it on the monitor for the group discussion.
  7. To close the monitor, press ✕ Exit at its top right.

Only one device? Tap Only one device? Open both on this screen above; drag the monitor window onto the projector.

What you can do

12 ready scenariosVF, asystole, PEA, SVT, bradycardia, sepsis, dengue, asthma, anaphylaxis, scorpion sting, hyperkalaemia
Realistic monitorHR counts compressions during CPR, EtCO₂ follows CPR quality and jumps on ROSC, PEA gives no rhythm alarm
Exam modeAlarms say only "ALARM", so the team must read the rhythm themselves
Automated debriefKey times, CPR fraction, hands-off time, checklist score and a timeline you can print or save as PDF

⚠️ Disclaimer

  • For education and simulation training only. Not a medical device. Do not use it to monitor, diagnose or treat real patients.
  • All waveforms and numbers are simulated and may not behave like a real patient or monitor.
  • Doses, energies and scenario content are teaching aids. Verify against current AHA PALS / IAP guidance and your local protocol.
