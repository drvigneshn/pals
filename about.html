About PALS Companion

Version · created by Dr Vignesh N, paediatrician

PALS Companion is a two-screen simulation tool for paediatric resuscitation teaching. A laptop, tablet or projector shows a realistic simulated bedside monitor; the instructor runs the case live from a phone. It is built for PALS-style courses, mock codes, simulation labs, and residency and nursing teaching.

How to use it

  1. On the laptop/projector, open pals.pediaos.com and choose Monitor screen.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone (or choose Instructor controls and type the 4-letter code).
  3. Tap Start monitor on the laptop to enable sound.
  4. Pick a scenario on the phone, read the stem, press Start, and move through the stages.
  5. Use Live for instant changes and ⚡ Surprise complications; open Debrief at the end.

Features

  • ECG, pleth, capnography and respiration waveforms; HR, SpO₂, EtCO₂, RR, NIBP, temperature; age-based alarms and sound.
  • Rhythms: sinus, SVT, 1st/2nd/3rd-degree AV block, VT with and without pulse, torsades, coarse/fine VF, asystole, PEA.
  • Physiology that teaches: HR counts compressions during CPR, EtCO₂ follows CPR quality and rises on ROSC, PEA gives no rhythm alarm, SpO₂ lags and loses its signal without a pulse.
  • 12 staged scenarios with instructor-only cues, weight-based doses and expected-action checklists.
  • Quick actions, team-event logging, ⚡ Surprise complications, exam mode, CPR feedback panel.
  • Automated debrief: time to CPR, first shock, adrenaline timing, CPR fraction, hands-off time, timeline; show on the monitor, copy, or print/save as PDF.
  • No accounts or installation; works offline once opened.
